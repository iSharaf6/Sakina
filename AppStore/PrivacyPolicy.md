# Haneen Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026

Haneen is developed by Islam Sharaf. Haneen does not include advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking or sale of personal data.

## Local data
Notes, bookmarks, reading preferences, prayer settings and reminder preferences are stored on your device. Widgets receive prayer times, a city-level label and time zone through Apple's App Group storage, without latitude or longitude.

## Accounts and optional backup
Apple, Google and email sign-in use Supabase Authentication. Supabase stores the account identifier, email address and display name where supplied, and authentication information needed to operate the account. Signing in alone does not upload local notes or bookmarks. You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account; this does not delete your separate local library.

A separate, optional Google Drive connection stores a private app-data backup of bookmarks and reflections. It is used only after you choose to connect it and does not include prayer coordinates. You can disconnect this backup in Settings.

## Location
Prayer times and Qibla calculations run on device. Apple location services may resolve a place name. If you open nearby mosques or halal places, the search location is shared with Apple Maps and OpenStreetMap Overpass services to find results. You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings.

## Network services
Recitation streams from EveryAyah. Source links open Quran.com, Sunnah.com or other cited references. Where configured, public scholarly content is read from Supabase. These services receive ordinary network information, including the IP address, and apply their own privacy policies.

## Your choices and retention
Delete notes and bookmarks inside Haneen, delete your sign-in account from Account settings, disconnect optional backup, and control location and notifications in iOS Settings. Local data is removed when the app is deleted, subject to device backups. Account data is retained to provide sign-in until the account is deleted; service security logs follow the providers' retention policies.
