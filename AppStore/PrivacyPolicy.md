# Yaqeen Privacy Policy

Effective 11 August 2026

> Before publishing this page, add the operator’s legal name, support email, and public Support URL.

Yaqeen is designed to be private by default. It does not contain advertising or cross-app tracking, and the developer does not sell personal data.

## Data stored on your device

Bookmarks, written reflections, language preferences, prayer settings, and notification preferences are stored locally on your device. Apple’s App Group storage lets the Yaqeen widget read calculated prayer times, a city-level label, and a time zone. Latitude and longitude are not included in the widget payload.

## Location and Qibla

Yaqeen requests location only after you choose a location-based feature. The location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Qibla location and heading readings are transient. Prayer calculations happen on device. Apple’s location services may provide a city-level place name. Yaqeen does not retain coordinates in its shared widget data.

You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings. Prayer times can also be configured using a selected city where that option is available.

## Optional backup

Some configured editions of Yaqeen can offer optional Google sign-in and a private Google Drive app-data backup. No Google account data is requested unless that option is visible and you choose to connect it. The backup contains bookmarks and reflections, not prayer coordinates. Google processes sign-in and backup data under its own privacy terms. You can sign out or disconnect access from Settings.

## Network services and source links

When the scholar-content service is configured, Yaqeen reads public verified-scholar profiles and published scholarly insights from Supabase. The public app does not send your bookmarks, reflections, searches, prayer location, Google account, or backup data to the scholar service.

When you play a recitation, audio is streamed from EveryAyah. Source links you choose to open may take you to Quran.com, Sunnah.com, or a cited scholarly reference. Supabase and those linked services receive ordinary network information, such as an IP address and request details, and apply their own privacy terms.

## Notifications

If you enable the daily guidance reminder, iOS schedules it locally. You can turn it off in Yaqeen or in iOS Settings.

## Your choices and deletion

You can remove bookmarks and reflections inside the app, disable notifications, deny location access, and disconnect any configured backup. Deleting Yaqeen removes its local app data, subject to any device backup retained by Apple and any optional external backup you previously created.

## Children

Yaqeen is a general-audience religious reflection app and is not directed at collecting information from children. It does not require an account in its default configuration.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by an updated effective date. Questions or deletion requests concerning an optional connected service can be sent to the support contact published with the app.
