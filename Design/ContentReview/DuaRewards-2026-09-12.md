# Du’a reward source audit — 12 September 2026

39 sourced notes cover 53 catalog entries. English and Arabic cards show the virtue, its conditions, the named source and its stated grading. Summaries were cross-checked against the linked Qur’an or hadith text; this is a source audit, not a claim of review or endorsement by the Sheikh.

Separate narrations are retained for morning/evening, bedtime and post-prayer Ayat al-Kursi. The three protective surahs are described together where the narration requires all three. Rewards requiring an entire surah or passage are not assigned to an excerpt alone. No unsupported repetition-based reward is added to the disputed seven-times Hasbiyallah report.

Fixed four old source URL forms in DuaLibrary: Muslim 2709a → 2708b (the hosted evening scorpion narration), 597 → 597a, 2137 → 2137a, and 2726 → 2726a.

## Sources and scope

### prophetic-master-repentance

Paradise is promised to the person who says this with conviction, then dies before evening or before morning.

**Conditions:** During the day or night, with firm conviction in its meaning.

**Evidence:** [Sahih al-Bukhari 6306](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6306). Sahih by collection.

**Shown for:** `prophetic-master-repentance`, `morning-sayyid-al-istighfar`.

### morning-ayat-al-kursi

Protection from the jinn until evening when recited in the morning, and until morning when recited in the evening.

**Conditions:** Recite the complete Ayat al-Kursi in the morning and evening.

**Evidence:** [Al-Hakim 1/562 · Hisn al-Muslim 75](https://sunnah.com/hisn:75). Authenticated by al-Albani, as cited in Hisn al-Muslim.

**Shown for:** `morning-ayat-al-kursi`.

### morning-surah-al-ikhlas

The Prophet ﷺ taught that these three surahs suffice the reader against everything.

**Conditions:** Read al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq and an-Nas together, three times each, morning and evening. This virtue concerns all three surahs.

**Evidence:** [Sunan Abi Dawud 5082](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:5082). Hasan (al-Albani).

**Shown for:** `morning-surah-al-ikhlas`, `morning-surah-al-falaq`, `morning-surah-an-nas`.

### morning-bismillah-la-yadurr

Protection from sudden affliction until evening or until morning is reported for this remembrance.

**Conditions:** Three times in the morning and three times in the evening.

**Evidence:** [Sunan Abi Dawud 5088](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:5088). Sahih (al-Albani).

**Shown for:** `morning-bismillah-la-yadurr`.

### evening-kalimat-allah-tammat

The Prophet ﷺ told a man stung by a scorpion that this evening remembrance would have protected him from that harm.

**Conditions:** In the evening. This Muslim narration does not specify a repetition count.

**Evidence:** [Sahih Muslim 2709a (with 2708b)](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2708b). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `evening-kalimat-allah-tammat`.

### morning-subhanallahi-wa-bihamdihi

On the Day of Resurrection, no one brings better than this except someone who says the same or more.

**Conditions:** One hundred times in the morning and one hundred times in the evening.

**Evidence:** [Sahih Muslim 2692](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2692). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `morning-subhanallahi-wa-bihamdihi`.

### morning-la-ilaha-illallah-100

The reward of freeing ten slaves, one hundred good deeds, one hundred sins erased, and protection from Satan until evening.

**Conditions:** One hundred times in a day, using the complete formula shown.

**Evidence:** [Sahih al-Bukhari 3293](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3293). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `morning-la-ilaha-illallah-100`, `anytime-tahlil-hundred`.

### evening-raditu-billah

This narration reports Allah’s promise to please the person who says these words in the evening.

**Conditions:** In the evening. Al-Tirmidhi calls it hasan gharib; this narration gives no repetition count.

**Evidence:** [Jami‘ at-Tirmidhi 3389](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3389). Hasan gharib (al-Tirmidhi).

**Shown for:** `evening-raditu-billah`.

### sleep-ayat-al-kursi

A guardian from Allah remains with the reader, and Satan does not approach until morning.

**Conditions:** Recite Ayat al-Kursi, 2:255, completely at bedtime. The stated virtue is for that ayah.

**Evidence:** [Sahih al-Bukhari 2311](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2311). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `sleep-ayat-al-kursi`, `ruqyah-quran-2-255-257`, `healing-ayat-al-kursi`.

### sleep-last-two-ayat-baqarah

The Prophet ﷺ said the last two ayat of al-Baqarah suffice whoever recites them at night.

**Conditions:** Read both 2:285 and 2:286 at night. The narration does not restrict this to the moment of sleep.

**Evidence:** [Sahih al-Bukhari 5009](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5009). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `sleep-last-two-ayat-baqarah`, `ruqyah-quran-2-284-286`.

### sleep-aslamtu-nafsi

Whoever dies that night after following this teaching dies upon the natural faith.

**Conditions:** Make wudu, lie on the right side, and make these words the last spoken before sleep.

**Evidence:** [Sahih al-Bukhari 247](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:247). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `sleep-aslamtu-nafsi`.

**Conditions:** At bedtime: SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times, Allahu Akbar 34 times.

**Evidence:** [Sahih al-Bukhari 5362](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5362). Sahih.

**Shown for:** `sleep-tasbih-fatima`.

### sleep-waking-at-night

### afterSalah-tasbih-thirty-three
