## Prompt set

### 01-reading-companion

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a single square iOS app icon for Haneen, an Islamic Qur'an and daily remembrance app. Make a genuinely NEW composition, using the attached existing icon ONLY as a reference for the identity of the cream cat with chocolate ears and one dark eye patch, tiny olive scarf, and the coloured-pencil illustration language. Do not repeat its diagonal oversized wooden stand. Refine the drawing into a confident, memorable, unified silhouette readable at 60 pixels. Warm solid opaque ivory background #FCF3E3 fills the entire square. Restrained olive green, cream, dark brown and antique gold. Clean intentional outlines with light hand-drawn texture, much less tiny scratchy detail. Friendly and peaceful, not babyish, not a generic pet app. The Qur'an must have equal or greater visual prominence to the cat. The cat is never on top of the Qur'an or on its pages. No lettering, pseudo-Arabic, text, crescent, star, extra props, outer border, rounded tile mask, 3D gloss, gradients, shadows, or watermark. One centered composition, generous 9% safe margin, fully square artwork, no layout sheet.
Composition A — READING COMPANION: front-facing close-up of the character, its large softly tilted head centered in the upper half, alert kind dot eyes making contact with the viewer. In the lower half, a large OPEN Qur'an with visible dark olive cover and a simple gold ornamental border, warm ivory pages suggested with only 2–3 subtle ruled strokes per page, no writing. The open book and face nest together as one compact nearly circular emblem. A very small antique-gold rehal cradle supports the book, its legs barely visible and never dominant. Only head, shoulders and a small curled tail tip at the side; no sprawling full body. Ears and book corners create a distinctive simple silhouette. Cat paws stay behind the book beside the cradle. Make the open book unmistakably central, wide and dignified.
