Your daily companion.

Prayer, Qur’an and du’a. Together.

Read the Qur’an in a traditional mushaf or a flexible digital reader. Keep local prayer times nearby, find du’a for the moment, and make room for a little dhikr.

A place to return to

Choose light or dark mode, add a widget, and pick the reminders that suit your day. Your reading notes and bookmarks stay on your device unless you choose an available backup option.

Your account

Yaqeen uses Supabase for account authentication with Apple, Google or email. Sign-in is separate from your local reading library.
