Your privacy.

Last updated 11 September 2026

This policy covers the Yaqeen iPhone app, developed by Islam Sharaf. Contact islamsharaf2005@gmail.com with privacy questions or account-deletion requests.

Account sign-in

When you sign in, Supabase processes your account identifier, email address and authentication credentials or provider tokens. Apple and Google may share your name, email address and provider identifier according to the permissions you grant. Apple’s private relay email can be used. Passwords for Apple or Google are entered with those providers; Yaqeen does not receive them.

Supabase stores the account and session information needed to authenticate you, including security and authentication logs that may contain IP addresses and device information. The Yaqeen Supabase project is hosted in Sydney, Australia; providers may also process information in other countries under their own policies.

Reading and personal library

Bookmarks, favourites, highlights, notes, reading preferences and progress are stored on your device. Signing in to a Yaqeen account does not automatically upload this library. If you explicitly use the separate Google Drive backup option in Settings, the app sends your selected backup data to your Google Drive app-data storage. Device backups are also governed by your Apple device settings.

Location and nearby places

With your permission, the app uses your location to calculate prayer times and Qibla on your device. Exact coordinates are not included in widget payloads. When you search for nearby places or open directions, Apple Maps or other map services used by the feature may receive the location and search information required to provide those results.

Audio, content and notifications

Online recitations and content updates contact their hosting services, which can receive normal connection information such as your IP address. Optional reminders are scheduled locally using your chosen settings. You can turn them off in Yaqeen or iOS Settings.

No advertising tracking

Yaqeen does not include advertising trackers or sell your personal data. Authentication and content service logs are used to operate those services, not to build advertising profiles.

Your choices and deletion

You can change permissions in iOS Settings and sign out in Yaqeen. To delete your sign-in account, open Settings → Account → Delete account, or contact the address above. Account deletion removes the Supabase sign-in account; it does not erase local notes or copies you placed in a backup service. Local app data can be removed by deleting the app, and cloud backups must be managed in the relevant service. Security logs and provider-held data may be retained under those services’ retention policies.

Service providers

See the privacy policies of Supabase, Apple and Google for information about their processing.

Changes

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date.
